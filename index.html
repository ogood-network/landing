Open source · Open network for good

Oh good, volunteers are here!

Ogood is an open-source, open network for good — a federation protocol that connects independent volunteer platforms so opportunities, skills, and people flow freely across organizations. Built on the Volunteering Data Standard, extended with ActivityPub federation and two-way matching.

VolunteerMatch × ActivityPub × Tinder

Three ideas, one open protocol — built on the Volunteering Data Standard ontology. Ogood = "Oh good, volunteers are here!" + open source, open network for good.

📋

VolunteerMatch

A marketplace of volunteer opportunities — but instead of one company owning the listings, every org hosts their own and they all interconnect.

🔗

ActivityPub

Federation done right. Any Ogood-compatible server can talk to any other. No silos, no lock-in, no central authority. Your data stays yours.

💛

Tinder (for mutual aid)

Two-way matching between volunteers and opportunities based on skills, interests, location, and availability. Swipe right on making a difference.

Built on existing standards

Ogood doesn't reinvent the wheel. It stands on the shoulders of established open standards.

📐

Volunteering Data Standard

Ogood's data model extends the Volunteering Data Standard ontology — the open vocabulary for describing volunteer opportunities, organizations, skills, and commitments. We add federation and matching, not a competing schema.

📡

ActivityPub (W3C)

The same federation protocol that powers Mastodon and the Fediverse. Ogood extends ActivityPub with volunteer-specific activity types — ExpressInterest, MatchVolunteer, LogHours — so any compatible server can federate.

How Ogood works

A federated network where every node is sovereign but connected.

1

Host your node

Any nonprofit, mutual aid group, or community org runs an Ogood server. They publish their own volunteer opportunities and manage their own members — no central platform required.

2

Federate freely

Nodes discover and talk to each other using the Ogood protocol (built on ActivityPub). Opportunities posted on one server appear across the network. Volunteers follow orgs the way you follow accounts on Mastodon.

3

Match intelligently

Volunteers build a portable profile of skills, interests, and availability. The protocol enables two-way matching — orgs find the right people, and people find the right roles, across the entire federation.

Built for the open web

Protocol-first, community-governed, and designed to outlast any single company.

🌐

Federated by design

Built on ActivityPub. Every Ogood node is independent — no single point of failure, no central database of volunteers, no platform decay.

🤝

Two-way matching

Volunteers express interest in roles; organizations express interest in volunteers. The protocol surfaces mutual matches across the entire network.

🪪

Portable volunteer identity

Your volunteer profile, hours logged, and endorsements travel with you across nodes. Switch servers without losing your history.

🔓

Open source & open spec

Extends the open Volunteering Data Standard ontology. The Ogood protocol spec is open. Reference implementations are open source. Anyone can build a client, a server, or a tool on top of it.

📍

Geo-aware discovery

Find volunteer opportunities near you — or remote ones — across every federated node. Location is first-class in the protocol.

⏱️

Verified hours & impact

Cryptographically signed volunteer hours that are portable and verifiable across the federation. No more spreadsheets and PDFs.
